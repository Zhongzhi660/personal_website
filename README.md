# Zhongzhi Li (李忠智)

I'm a Ph.D. student in the [School of Computing](https://computing.uga.edu/) at the University of Georgia, advised by [Dr. Ninghao Liu](https://ninghaohello.github.io/). I received my M.S. from Fudan University.

My research aims to build AI agents that can reliably carry out long-horizon tasks in real computing environments. To this end, I develop methods that synthesize verifiable tasks and environments at scale and use them to post-train agents with reinforcement learning. More broadly, I am interested in understanding and steering foundation models from the inside, through interpretability, controllability, and safety.
