# Zhongzhi Li (李忠智)

I'm a Ph.D. student in the [School of Computing](https://computing.uga.edu/) at the University of Georgia, advised by [Dr. Ninghao Liu](https://ninghaohello.github.io/). I received my M.S. from Fudan University.

My research aims to build AI agents that reliably complete long-horizon tasks, by scaling verifiable task synthesis and post-training agents with reinforcement learning; more broadly, I study how to make foundation models interpretable, controllable, and safe.
